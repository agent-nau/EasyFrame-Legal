How it works

Three steps from Figma frame to Studio GUI.

Design in Figma

Select a frame and run the EasyFrame plugin. Click “Generate Code.”

Paste in Studio

Open the EasyFrame plugin in Roblox Studio and enter the 6-character code.

Done

Your UI appears instantly as native Roblox instances. Fully editable.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

EasyFrame is a local desktop application for Windows that connects Figma and Roblox Studio. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Data we collect

None. EasyFrame does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer or to any external server we operate. We run no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

How your designs are processed

When you generate a code in the Figma plugin, your selected frame is sent to the EasyFrame app running on your own computer. The Roblox Studio plugin then retrieves the converted design from that same local app. Design data is not sent anywhere else.

Roblox login and image uploads

If you choose to log in with Roblox, authentication happens on Roblox’s own website through OAuth. EasyFrame never sees your password. The access token Roblox issues is stored locally on your device and is used only to upload images from your designs to your Roblox account. Uploaded images are sent to Roblox and are subject to the Roblox Privacy Policy. You can log out at any time and delete the locally stored token.

Third-party services

Figma and Roblox Studio are separate products with their own privacy policies. Downloading EasyFrame from GitHub is subject to GitHub’s policies. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Children

EasyFrame collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Use of Roblox accounts is governed by Roblox’s own age requirements.
