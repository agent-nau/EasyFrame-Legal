# EasyFrame Privacy Policy

EasyFrame is a local desktop application. 

1. **Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to external servers. 
2. **Roblox Authentication:** When you log in via Roblox, your User ID and Username are saved locally on your own computer in a file (`~/.easyframe/config.json`) solely for the purpose of verifying your identity within the local bridge. 
3. **Design Data:** Any Figma designs or UI data you transfer are processed locally on your machine and are not stored on any remote database.

By using EasyFrame, you agree to this local-only data processing.
